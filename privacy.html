1. Who We Are

Onew4n Photography (“Onew4n”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a professional photography studio offering portrait, wedding, event, editorial and commercial photography. We operate this website and are responsible for the personal information you share with us through it.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that helps us respond to you and deliver our services:

TypeExamplesSource
Contact detailsName, email, phone numberContact form, email, phone
Booking detailsSession type, date, location, project notesContact form, consultations
Billing informationBilling address, payment recordsInvoices and payment providers

We do not store full card numbers — payments are processed securely by third-party payment providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To plan, schedule and deliver photography sessions and galleries.
  • To send invoices, contracts and service-related messages.
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it.
  • To send occasional news or offers — only if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent, the performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in running our business, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Your Images

Photographs are personal, and we treat them with care:

  • Client galleries are private and password-protected, shared only with you and the people you choose.
  • We will only feature your images in our portfolio, website or social media with your permission, as agreed in your contract or in writing.
  • You can withdraw permission for future portfolio use at any time by contacting us.
  • We never sell your images or license them to third parties without your explicit consent.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use a small number of cookies to keep it working properly and to collect anonymous usage statistics. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, such as:

  • Online gallery and cloud storage providers.
  • Email, scheduling and form-handling services.
  • Payment processors and accounting software.
  • Print labs, when you order prints or albums.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

These providers may only use your information to perform services for us and must keep it secure.

7. Data Retention

We keep enquiry information for up to 12 months if no booking is made. Client records and invoices are kept as long as required for legal and tax purposes. Final images are archived for at least 2 years after delivery so we can help if you lose your copies, but we recommend keeping your own backups.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encrypted connections, password-protected galleries and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including marketing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online. Photographs of minors are only taken with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the “Last updated” date shown at the top.
