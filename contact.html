Before You Ask

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 6–12 months ahead is ideal. Portrait and commercial sessions can usually be scheduled within 2–4 weeks.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Local travel is included in most packages, and we happily travel nationally and internationally — travel costs are quoted upfront.

How long until I receive my photos?

Portrait galleries are delivered within 2 weeks; weddings and larger projects within 4–6 weeks. A sneak peek is shared within 72 hours.

Is a deposit required?

A 30% retainer secures your date, with the balance due before the session or wedding day.
